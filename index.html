Clocks don't all change on the same day.

The US changes clocks two weeks before Europe. Chile and Brazil move the other way, or not at all. Arizona never does. A weekly meeting that works in January can land at dinner time in March.

Fusos checks every meeting date. When someone's clocks change, you see the new local times before anyone has to ask.

  • One board per stretch between clock changes, including the awkward weeks when one country has changed and another hasn't.
  • Uses the official time zone database in your browser, date by date. Never fixed offsets.
  • Shows exactly whose working hours a time falls outside, and by how much.

Text ready for your email.
