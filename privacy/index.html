Privacy policy

Last updated November 11, 2026

In short: Fusos doesn't collect, store, sell or share your personal data. There's no account, no analytics and no advertising. What you enter stays in your browser, on your device.

The Fusos Chrome extension

Fusos helps you find meeting times across time zones. To do that you enter people's names or nicknames, their time zones, their working hours and days, and the times you propose. You can also save a group as a team.

  • Where it's kept: in the extension's storage inside your Chrome browser, on your device. It isn't sent to us or to anyone else, and we have no server that could receive it.
  • How to delete it: use "Start over" in Fusos, or remove the extension. Removing it deletes everything it stored.
  • Network: the extension works offline and doesn't load anything from the internet. Time zone rules come from your browser's built-in time zone database. The only requests it ever makes are the two described below, a licence check and your calendar's free/busy times, and both happen only after you ask for them.
  • Web pages: the extension reads a web page only when you ask it to. When you click "Fill from this email" (or "Fill Fusos from this email" in the right-click menu), it reads the visible text of the tab you are on, once, to find the people, places and dates of the meeting. That text is processed by Chrome's built-in model on your computer; it isn't sent anywhere and isn't stored. The extension can't read a tab unless you ask it to on that tab, and it never reads pages on its own.
  • Fill from text: text you paste into the "Fill from text" box is handled the same way: by Chrome's built-in model, on your computer, and then discarded.
  • Copying: when you press Copy, the email text (and, if you choose, a picture) goes to your clipboard so you can paste it. Nothing else happens with it.
  • Inserting: when you press "Insert into email" (or "Insert Fusos times here" in the right-click menu), the extension writes that same text and picture into the message you are editing in that tab. It reads nothing from the page and sends nothing.
  • Add to calendar: "Download .ics" creates a calendar file on your computer; "Google Calendar" opens Google's own page in a new tab with the meeting details in the link. Neither sends anything to us.

If a future version needs more access, this policy will be updated before that version is released, and Chrome will ask for your permission.

Your calendar

Fusos Pro can show the times already booked in your own Google Calendar on your row of the board. This is off until you press "Connect Google Calendar" in the extension and approve it on Google's consent screen. Fusos then asks Google's free/busy service (the calendar.freebusy scope, the narrowest Google offers) for your main calendar only, and only for the dates on the boards: when you connect, when those dates change, and when you press Refresh. The answer is only when each booked time starts and ends; Fusos never receives event titles, guests, descriptions or locations, because the free/busy service doesn't return them. The times are kept in the panel's memory while it is open; they are never saved in the browser, never sent to us or to anyone but Google, and never put into the email text. Press "Disconnect" (under More options) to end it: the extension forgets the token and asks Google to revoke it. You can also remove Fusos at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Google's own privacy policy applies to your use of Google Calendar.

Fusos's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

This website

fusos.app uses no cookies, no analytics and no third-party fonts or scripts. It's hosted on GitHub Pages, and GitHub may keep technical logs such as IP addresses for security; see the GitHub General Privacy Statement.

Payments

Fusos is free for up to three people. Fusos Pro, a one-time purchase, is sold by Lemon Squeezy, our merchant of record. When you buy, Lemon Squeezy's checkout page (opened in a new tab) collects your email address and payment details and handles tax and receipts under its own privacy policy; Fusos never sees your card or your name. Lemon Squeezy emails you a licence key. When you enter that key in Fusos, the extension sends it, with the instance name "Fusos Chrome extension", to api.lemonsqueezy.com to activate it, and about once a week after that to confirm it is still valid; Lemon Squeezy answers with an instance id that Fusos keeps next to the key in this browser. That request carries nothing else: no participants, no text, no page content. Without a key, Fusos makes no licence request at all. Removing the licence (More options › Fusos Pro) tells Lemon Squeezy to free that activation and deletes the key from this browser.

Children
