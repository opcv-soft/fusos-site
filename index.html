Clocks don't all change on the same day.

The US changes clocks two weeks before Europe. Chile and Brazil move the other way, or not at all. Arizona never does. A weekly meeting that works in January can land at dinner time in March.

Fusos checks every meeting date. When someone's clocks change, you see the new local times before anyone has to ask.

  • One board per stretch between clock changes, including the awkward weeks when one country has changed and another hasn't.
  • Uses the official time zone database in your browser, date by date. Never fixed offsets.
  • Shows exactly whose working hours a time falls outside, and by how much.

Text ready for your email.

Pick a time and Fusos writes it out for everyone, in their own local time, with a flag for each place. Add a colour picture of everyone's day if you like.

In English, French, Spanish or Portuguese.

One click puts it in the email.

Fusos opens in Chrome's side panel, next to the email you're writing. Click Insert into email and the proposed times, and the picture, land in the message at the cursor. Or copy them and paste anywhere.

Start from the email you got.

With the message open, click Fill from this email, or right-click it and choose Fill Fusos from this email. Chrome's built-in model reads the message on your computer, and Fusos drafts the people, their places and the dates. You check the draft and apply it. Nothing is sent anywhere.

This needs Chrome 138 or later with Chrome's built-in model available on your machine. Without it, the planner works just the same; you add people by hand.
